# Privacy

Louvain's privacy policy is at <https://louvain.ai/privacy>. This page covers what this plugin does.

## Data collection

- **The plugin collects nothing.** It is text: skills and one server address. It has no code that runs on your computer.
- **Louvain receives the arguments of the tool calls Claude makes** when you have connected Louvain, for example a question, a search phrase or a document id. It does not receive your conversation with Claude, Claude's memory or your chat history.

## Usage and storage

- **Questions and answers.** Every question and answer is kept in your answer history. The words of a question are shown only to you. Your workspace's administrators see how answering is going (counts, refusal reasons, response times, feedback) without the words. See "Access within your organisation" in the privacy policy.
- **Saved files.** Files Claude saves because you asked it to are stored in the space they were saved to. They are labelled as written by an agent, and only that space's readers can see them.
- **Connection records.** When a connection was approved, when it was last used and when it ended are recorded in the workspace's audit log.
- **Credentials.** Sign-in uses OAuth. Claude holds the access token in its own secure storage; this plugin never sees it.

## Third-party sharing

Louvain does not sell or share your data. The subprocessors that host and run the service are listed at <https://louvain.ai/subprocessors>.

## Data retention

Answer history and saved files are kept for as long as your workspace keeps them. Your workspace's administrators and Louvain's policy decide that; see <https://louvain.ai/privacy>.

Disconnecting an agent ends its access at once. Files it saved are kept unless you choose to remove them when you disconnect it.
